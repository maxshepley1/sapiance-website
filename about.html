About

Sapiance Capital is an investment management company active in direct lending strategies backed by real assets.

Through an innovative approach combining financial structuring and real assets, Sapiance Capital capture the current lack of lending supply from financial institutions, replacing traditional sources of capital. We are very active in funding property portfolios, as well as residential bridge and development loan strategies across Europe. We have diversified our investments towards Corporate secured funding across Europe and across sectors (SaaS, Distribution rights, commodity business, portfolio companies). Our strategies are short to medium-term with an average typical duration of 24 months, ranging from as short as 6 months to up to 4 years.

We mainly act on behalf of single family offices, UHNWIs as well as niche institutional investors.
