Strategies

Our main investment strategy is to capture the current dislocation in the short term (2 years) real estate lending sector to deliver our investors attractive risk-adjusted returns via various strategies.

  • Senior Lending
  • Sectors: Real estate, Growth companies, IP (Brands, Patents, Copyright, distribution rights), commodity trading activities, holding companies
  • Repurchase agreements
  • Development Financing
  • Secured Bridge financing

We look at properties across Commercial, Residential and Hospitality sectors in Europe, predominantly in London.

  • Typical loan term 6–30 months
  • Typical loan size GBP 5–50 millions

We typically work with experienced property entrepreneurs or asset owners looking for efficient and straightforward financing.

Our investors are typically single family offices, high net worth individuals and institutional investors.
